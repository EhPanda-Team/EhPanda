# Sketch Manifest

## Design direction

Preserve the established Home framework while exploring richer section information and sizes
for larger iPad windows and a future macOS app. The owner’s brief is the reference; no Mac App
Store inspection or redesign of app navigation is required. D98 selects C for the slideshow, while the remaining sections retain the B preference.
D104 reopens landscape slideshow exploration with portrait preserved; no replacement is selected. D99–D100 implement the selected B+C mix and
shared simplified titles as a native iOS review trial; see [the quick summary](../quick/261017-pjj-implement-b-home-sections-with-selected-/261017-pjj-SUMMARY.md).
