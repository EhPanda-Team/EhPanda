# Sketch Manifest

## Design direction

Preserve the established Home framework while exploring richer section information and sizes
for larger iPad windows and a future macOS app. The owner’s brief is the reference; no Mac App
Store inspection or redesign of app navigation is required. B is the current preference, with C-style slideshow grouping only in ample space; the revised
presentation awaits review and is not approved for implementation.
