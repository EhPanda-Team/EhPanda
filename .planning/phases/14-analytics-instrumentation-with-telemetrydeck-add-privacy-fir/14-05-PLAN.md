<objective>
Define `AnalyticsSignal` — the closed enum that is the entire analytics vocabulary available to reducers — and the internal rendering layer that turns each case into a signal name and a flat parameter dictionary.

Purpose: this is the load-bearing half of D-09. `AnalyticsClient.send` will accept nothing but this type, and rendering is the only place in the whole codebase where an analytics string is created. Everything a reducer can express is enumerated here; anything not enumerated here is unexpressible.
Output: the signal enum, its rendering, and an exhaustive test suite including the reserved-key collision check and the no-content negative sweep.
</objective>
