<objective>
Instrument the search family: performed searches, filter and quick-search panels across the Search, Search-root and Favorites screens, quick-search word selection, and the Favorites gallery-detail push — and give the search family a test target, since it has none today.

Purpose: this is the highest-leakage-risk module in the phase. The search-history action carries the raw keyword, the quick-search word carries user-authored text, and the tag-tap path carries tag values. D-06 forbids all three, and the only thing standing between them and the wire is where the emission is placed and which type it hands over.
Output: seven emission sites, one new reducer action, and two new test targets registered in the default plan.
</objective>
