<objective>
Instrument `DownloadsFeature`: download completion and failure as edge-triggered transitions, plus delete, move, and the downloads-list gallery-detail push.

Purpose: this is the least clean seam in the phase and research flagged it as needing a task of its own. The downloads list learns about progress through a stream of **full snapshots**, so the obvious emission fires on every observation tick and reports one finished download hundreds of times. The fix is the transition-diff technique the haptics reducer extension already uses in this repository: compare the previous snapshot against the new one and emit only on the edge.
Output: five emission sites, one of them edge-triggered, with the double-count and cold-start cases pinned by test.
</objective>
