<objective>
Emit one reader-session signal at reader dismissal, carrying bucketed pages-read and bucketed session duration.

Purpose: D-05 family 3's reader session. The shape is a discretionary decision made here — a **single end-of-session signal**, because a start signal without a matching end is unanalyzable, because the vendor bills per signal, and because the dismissal case is already the module's synchronous teardown seam and therefore the one place both facts are still available.
Output: per-session tracking state, one emission site, and a clock-driven `TestStore` proof.
</objective>
