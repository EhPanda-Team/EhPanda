Close G-15-11 (warning, blocking under this project's perfection bar; violates SC4's intent).
Nine session-lifecycle mutators are `public` with zero production callers outside DownloadClient —
public solely for the cross-module test target. Any module linking DownloadClient can call
`markContinuedSessionEnded(sessionID:)` or `pauseAllSchedulable(expiring:)` and detach or cancel
the live session without going through the coordinator's own choreography. The module already
carries the correct pattern (`DownloadClient+Testing.swift`: `#if DEBUG` + `testing` prefix), and
15-26 already accepted this finding class once by making `prepareWorkingSeed` private.

The fix: drop all nine to internal, and route the six mutators the suites genuinely call through
new thin `#if DEBUG` forwarders. The three with zero test callers get NO forwarder.

**This plan also performs the round's mandatory headroom relocation.**
`DownloadContinuedSessionTests.swift` sits at 999 lines against the 1000-line `file_length` ERROR
gate, and this plan's re-spell adds characters to ~30 of its lines — any wrap past the 120-column
limit adds a line and breaks the build. Per the round contract, a plan touching that file must
move a case out first. The expiration-and-teardown case family (the cases from
`testExpirationLeavesTheQueueInThePerGalleryPauseBaselineState` at `:602` to the end of the
suite, plus the private helpers only they use) moves whole to a new
`DownloadContinuedSessionExpirationTests.swift`. This is a relocation, not a rewrite: byte-identical
bodies, no helper duplicated, shared helpers lifted to `DownloadFeatureTestHelpers.swift` per the
15-26 helper-lift precedent. The relocation ALSO unblocks plans 15-29 through 15-32, which touch
these files again. (G-15-12's IN-05 records this move as its headroom half; its remaining items
land in 15-32.)

**Sequencing note (mandated):** G-15-7's regressions in plan 15-29 drive the announcing
preparation directly and therefore need whichever seam this plan chooses. That is why this plan
precedes 15-29, and why `testingPrepareWorkingSeedAnnouncingProgress` is among the six.

Purpose: the capability's mutation surface is module-internal, tests reach it through one
`#if DEBUG` seam that adds nothing between suite and coordinator, and the file-length gate stops
hanging over every later plan in this round.
Output: nine access drops, six forwarders, the relocation, the full re-spell sweep (~100 call
sites across ten test files), and a green full run.
