<objective>
Close G-15-7 (blocker; defeats SC2, and SC1 through D-11's stall-expiration consequence — a
frozen numerator is the maximally stalled reading the scheduler force-expires first, and the
`.expired` arm pauses EVERY schedulable download).

15-26's D-G6-01 attached the floor withdrawal to the blanking LOOP inside
`reconcileWorkingManifestAgainstPageFiles` and asserted, in two doc comments, that the blanking
is the accounting basis's only deliberate downward mover. That premise is false in source. At
least four deliberate movers exist, three inside the very function the withdrawal lives in:

1. `setupWorkingFolder` deletes the working folder when `shouldReuseWorkingFolder` is false
   (`.redownload` / `.update`), so no seed is materialized.
2. `ensureWorkingManifest` then writes a fresh all-empty manifest and calls
   `updateDownloadIndex` (`+ExecutionSupport.swift:420`) — the record the numerator is summed
   from drops to 0 of N.
3. The reconciliation is reached with that all-empty manifest, its loop blanks nothing,
   `blankedPageCount == 0` returns early — BEFORE the counted-basis test and the withdrawal.
   Nothing is withdrawn.
4. `writeInitialManifest` (`+PublicAPI.swift`) writes a fresh all-empty manifest and re-indexes
   whenever `reusableExistingManifest` returns nil — the enqueue route's version of the same
   movement.

The route is a first-class UI action and the action that starts the session: `DetailView` sends
`.retryDownloadButtonTapped(.redownload)` for an errored gallery with ANY downloaded pages
(`downloadNeedsRepair` requires `completedPageCount == 0`), the record with 0 < C < N is counted
RAW by D-G4-01's first half, the card opens at C / N with the floor seeded to C, the spawned run
wipes the record to 0 of N after its network fetch, withdraws nothing, and every one of the next
C page flushes pushes the same C. The verification's own staging analysis confirms the freeze is
reachable single-gallery AND multi-gallery, and that the `validatedManifest` mismatch (page count
changed upstream, or a truncated manifest) and the `.update`-of-a-trusted-gallery are two further
instances of the same root cause.

**D-G7-01, the rule this plan installs: every deliberate downward movement of the session
accounting basis withdraws its counted portion from the floor, keyed on the pre/post
`downloadIndex[gid]` delta — never on a named mechanism.** Per the gap's mandated shape:

- A non-async bracket, `withdrawingCountedBasisMovement(gid:_:)`, reads the index record before
  the movement (its completed count, and the counted-basis predicate: record incomplete, or gid
  in `observedIncompleteSessionGIDs`), runs the movement closure, reads the record again, and —
  when `continuedSessionID != nil` and the basis was counting — subtracts
  `max(before − after, 0)` from `lastPushedCompletedPageCount`, unclamped, exactly as 15-26's
  contract requires. The whole stretch is synchronous, so no interleaved push can observe a
  lowered basis under an un-lowered floor.
- `prepareWorkingSeed` wraps its entire preparation in the bracket (movers 1–3 covered by
  construction, and any future mover that converges there). `writeInitialManifest` wraps its
  body in the same bracket (mover 4). The local withdrawal is REMOVED from
  `reconcileWorkingManifestAgainstPageFiles`.
- Reading the INDEX record on both sides subsumes WR-05: the withdrawal amount and the counted
  test are measured on the value the numerator is actually summed from, so working-manifest /
  index-record divergence (the re-slot-after-title-change path) cannot leave the floor holding a
  difference.
- Deletions are excluded: the retirement ledger already values departures from the honest
  record. The bracket's two sites never delete; the sweep proves the exclusion holds everywhere
  else.
- Both wrong single-mover doc paragraphs are corrected, the seed comment and the scalar's
  declaration doc are re-pointed at D-G7-01, and 15-26-SUMMARY's Table 2 row 5 disposition
  ("fresh all-empty manifests — nothing changed, nothing withdrawn"), which is wrong for every
  counted record, is superseded by this plan's Table (existing summaries are never edited).

Purpose: after this plan, no deliberate coordinator movement of the basis — present or future,
enumerated or not — can freeze the card; the numerator dips once, honestly, by exactly the
counted portion, and every page of real work anywhere in the queue advances it again.
Output: the bracket with its two call sites, the corrected docs, three counted-record regressions
watched to fail first, the writer sweep verified row by row, and a green full run.
</objective>
