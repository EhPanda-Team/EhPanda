<objective>
Close CR-03 (verification gap 2's read-path half): `validate(download:verifiesContentHashes:discardingRejected:)` still defaults `discardingRejected` to TRUE, and both remaining read callers take the default. Opening a downloaded gallery runs `loadManifest` → `sanitizeLocalFilesIfNeeded` → `scanCompletedFolder`, whose two probes discard their results (`_ =`) and exist purely for the deleting side effect — a zero-byte or non-regular page file is destroyed by an ordinary read, the manifest keeps its non-empty hash, the gallery keeps deriving `.completed` under D-SSOT-07, and the divergence survives relaunch with nothing reconciling it. `resumeMode(for:)` reaches the same deletion deciding repair-versus-redownload. The justification at `DownloadStore+Operations.swift:494-498` ("answer feeds nothing destructive") applies the wrong test: the hazard is that FORMING the answer deletes files.

Establish the property by construction, per the verification's chosen option: flip the destructive default to FALSE across the whole parameter family so mutation is opt-in and every deleting caller must name it — never patch the two named callers alone. Sweep EVERY caller of `validate` / `existingPageRelativePaths` / `existingCoverRelativePath` / `pageFileScan` / `sanitizeAssetFileIfNeeded` / `imageURLs` / `localCoverURL` with a disposition.

Purpose: reads never destroy; the persisted manifest and the disk cannot be diverged by looking (SC2's display honesty, SC3's no-lost-work).
Output: opt-in mutation defaults, a deleted side-effect-only sweep, corrected contract docs, a full caller disposition table, and zero-byte read-path regressions.
</objective>
