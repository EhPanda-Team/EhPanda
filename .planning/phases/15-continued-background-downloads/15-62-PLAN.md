<objective>
Close CR-01 by making validation evidence gathering strictly non-mutating and moving every rejected-file deletion behind the combined wholesale authorization guard.

The manifest remains the persisted SSOT. Classification may identify absence, rejected structure/size, content mismatch, or an unanswerable file; only the combined positively-refuted set may authorize a durable correction, and authorization must precede both disk deletion and manifest blanking. This removes the current path where the verdict scan deletes first and the later guard refuses the matching write.

Purpose: ensure validation never creates record/disk disagreement during a refused reconciliation.
Output: rejected-page identity in `PageFileScan`, a non-mutating validation route, post-guard refuted-file removal, and boundary tests.
</objective>

## Artifacts this phase produces

- New `PageFileScan.rejectedPageRelativePaths: [Int: String]` classification member, with a defaulted initializer argument for existing constructed scans.
- New `DownloadStore.validate(download:verifiesContentHashes:discardingRejected:)` parameter, defaulting to historical behavior for non-validation callers while `validateImageData` passes `false` explicitly.
- Generalized `removeRefutedPageFiles(folderURL:pageRelativePaths:refutedPages:)` store operation replacing mismatch-only removal.
- New RED-first tests in `DownloadValidationReconciliationTests` for wholesale refusal and authorized partial rejection.
