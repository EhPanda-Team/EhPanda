## Why this plan is a redesign and not another patch

Phase 15 spent rounds 8 through 17 in a loop: gaps found, gap closure planned, the
closure's own fix opening the next round's gap. The verifier's own trail names the
generator precisely:

- Round 16: "a fix that corrects the PREDICATE and inherits the BASIS" (G-15-30's fix
  swapped a pinned 0/N for a pinned N/N).
- Round 18: "a fix that changes the BASIS and then gates it on a BRANCH — and the branch
  it chose is the record's own completeness, which is the one quantity the refusal family
  makes untrustworthy" (G-15-34's guard made the credit piecewise and non-monotonic:
  f(N−1) = N−1 > f(N) = N−d).

The root cause, one level below both formulations: **the session numerator was INFERRED
from the index record and then corrected, when the record is a reading of a different
quantity.** The record reports the gallery's cumulative on-disk state; the numerator
wants the progress of the work the session covers. The two agree only for honest
records, and the refusal family exists precisely because `reconcileWorkingManifestAgainstPageFiles`
sometimes must hand a lying manifest back verbatim. Every round's fix added a correction
term to the inference — the trust set (G-15-23), the run-owned proof (G-15-26), the
subtracted page debt (G-15-30), the completeness guard (G-15-34) — and every correction
term has a boundary where it switches on or off. Each boundary is a discontinuity in the
credited count, and each discontinuity is where the next round's defect lived. The loop
was structural: patching an inference of the wrong quantity mints new boundaries at
least as fast as rounds close them.

## The design

**Measure the quantity directly.** `DownloadCoordinator.RunProgressBasis` is a run-owned
measurement, announced at the run's own preparation and retired at its exit:

```
inheritedPages       pages the run inherits rather than performs, valued by evidence
initialPendingPages  the run's own to-do list at preparation
outstandingPages     the to-do subset not yet landed; only ever shrinks
creditedPageCount    |inheritedPages ∪ (initialPendingPages ∖ outstandingPages)|
```

While a run is live, its gallery's credit never consults the record. The count is
monotone by construction (a union of a fixed set with a growing set) and continuous
across the record completing (nothing in it reads the record) — the two properties whose
absence G-15-34 named, held structurally instead of argued branch by branch.

**`inheritedPages` follows the blanking loop's own positive-signal rule, applied to
credit.** Only positive absence zeroes a claim:

1. A successful listing is authoritative both ways: it yields the probed files, plus the
   claimed pages the per-file probe could not answer for (the same population the
   blanking loop refuses to blank).
2. A failed listing is a non-answer: the record's claims stand whole.
3. A COMPLETE-reading record forfeits the claims the run was asked to fetch — a repair
   or retry of a "finished" gallery is itself the route's assertion that those pages are
   bad. This is the round-18 corrective ("subtract only the owed pages the record still
   claims") absorbed as one case of a single evidence rule. An incomplete record's
   claims carry no such refutation; its to-do overlap comes only from the scan's own
   failure, and the credited union keeps any overlap from counting twice.

The evidence travels on `WorkingSeed` (`unprobedPages`, `scanSucceeded`) from the one
scan the preparation already took, so the credit rule and the blanking rule can never
answer from different probes.

**The session's remaining state becomes honest about what it is:**

- `observedIncompleteSessionGIDs` is now a pure observation set — seeded empty, written
  only by snapshot merges reading `isIncomplete`, never by proof-grants, never withdrawn
  at run exits. Its one job: decide what a complete-reading record with no run in flight
  contributes. Within one session a record moves from incomplete to complete only
  through landed pages, so the branch it opens has no route to an unearned number;
  G-15-30's granted-membership hazard is unreachable by construction.
- `sessionCreditedPages` is three regimes in consultation order: live basis; honest
  record (incomplete raw, or complete-after-observation raw); zero (D-G4-01's queued
  window). The regimes hand off continuously: at the announce the honest record's raw
  count equals the inherited evidence, and at a run exit it equals the final measurement
  because every landed page was flushed.
- `withdrawingCountedBasisMovement` (D-G7-01) now measures `sessionCreditedPages` around
  the movement instead of the raw record delta with a trust predicate. "Whoever lowers
  the counted basis withdraws" becomes true for movers no one enumerated — including a
  successor run's announcement replacing a superseded predecessor's basis, which no
  record reading could see. The announcement is its own sibling bracket; brackets never
  nest (the inner delta would be withdrawn twice).
- `flushManifestPageProgress` is now structurally the single landing point:
  `performCacheCapture` routes its restored page through it (G-15-35), so a capture
  during a live run advances the measurement exactly as a page-loop landing does. The
  orphaned single-page store overload is deleted.

**What was deliberately kept**, because seventeen rounds of verification proved the
shape right: the retirement ledger and its symmetric D-G2-01 formula, the run-exit
freeze that makes departure detection ordering-insensitive, the monotonic floor as
residual defence, the announce gate on the run's own pending list (G-15-27), and the
queued-window zero.

**Timing fidelity (G-15-36):** `BackgroundProcessingClient.noop` — the coordinator's
default client — now suspends at all three endpoints like every hand-built double, and
the double censuses walk the processing client's module so the rule is owned by a build
failure rather than honoured by classification.

**Doc closure (G-15-37):** 15-SECURITY.md's T-15-09, T-15-03 and the scheduler trust
boundary now record the 15-51 per-process identifier design.

**Test hygiene (G-15-38):** `landPageFiles` builds its payload through the shared
`pageResults`, restoring the "a naming change moves both together" guarantee.

## What the redesign changes observably, and why each change is the honest reading

- The incomplete-reading refusal family (a partially downloaded gallery whose claimed
  files are deleted through the Files app — round 18's derived production route) now
  pushes a series that climbs from the evidence: the record's refuted claims leave the
  numerator at the announce (excused from the floor by the announce bracket) and every
  landed page raises it. The old series froze at the claim and over-reported the
  terminal pair. New series test:
  `testAnIncompleteRefusalRepairsPushesClimbFromTheEvidence` — the incomplete-half
  SERIES observation round 18 demanded.
- A page-count-mismatch repair (record replaced by a fresh all-empty manifest while the
  page files survive) no longer dips to zero: the probed survivors are inherited work
  the run will not re-fetch. The old dip was record-inference, not truth.
- A scan-failure repair over an honest incomplete record keeps its claims credited (a
  non-answer is not authority to zero recorded work) — unchanged from the old behavior,
  now derived from the evidence rule rather than from branch-1 raw counting.
