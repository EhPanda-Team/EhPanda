<objective>
Close WR-04 and WR-05 (verification gap 4), the two unhandled consequences of the CR-04 narrowing. WR-04: `retryPagesDone(.failure)` clears `retryingPageIndices` and re-sends `.loadInspection` without setting `state.toast` (DownloadInspectorReducer.swift:189-193), even though the reducer owns a toast surface and uses it for validation results at line 235 — so the new refusal turns a Retry tap into a visible no-op; and `.failure(.notFound)` conflates 'gallery absent' (RetryHelpers.swift:73), 'folder absent' (line 88), and 'the pages you named are outside this gallery' (line 81), so no localized message can match what happened. WR-05: an empty-but-present selection that only becomes inadmissible at fetch time is not a no-op — `pendingPageIndices` returns nothing, the announcement gate declines, `downloadCoverImage` still runs, and `finalizeBatchResult` calls `missingFinalizedPageIndices` over the WHOLE manifest, so pages the `.repair` seed just blanked throw the generic incomplete-download error and the gallery settles into a persistent error record for work nobody requested.

One deliberate contract revision rides along: 15-64 made `normalizeFetchedPayload` preserve an explicitly empty selection so it could not widen to unrestricted work. This plan strengthens that same fail-closed stance — the collapse now fails LOUDLY at the boundary with a truthful reason instead of failing generically at finalize. The no-widening property is unchanged: an explicit selection still never becomes `nil`.

Purpose: refusals are visible and truthful; fetch-time drift cannot manufacture a false failure record (SC2).
Output: two distinct localized errors, a surfaced failure toast, a throwing collapse boundary, a swept three-state consumer table, and regressions at all three layers.
</objective>
