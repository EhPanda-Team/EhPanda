<objective>
Close G-15-6 (blocker, introduced by the 15-25 fix, violates SC2 and — through D-11's
stall-expiration consequence — SC1). `pushContinuedSessionProgress` floors the pushed numerator
with `max(lastPushedCompletedPageCount, sessionProgress.displayCompletedPageCount)`
(`DownloadClient+ContinuedSession.swift:563-567`) — a single scalar over the whole-queue SUMMED
numerator. Its correctness argument, written at `:524-529`, assumed the accounting basis could not
shrink. D-G5-01's `reconcileWorkingManifestAgainstPageFiles`
(`DownloadClient+ExecutionSupport.swift:321-340`) invalidated that premise: it is the first
mechanism in this phase that deliberately LOWERS an already-counted gallery's record mid-session
(blanking hashes for vanished files, on the `.repair` route, the `.initial` reuse route, and the
repair-seed materialization route alike). The floor cannot tell that deliberate correction from
the regression it was built to mask, so it absorbs the credit: after a correction, real work by
ANY gallery is invisible until the summed numerator climbs back over the pre-correction total.
The reachable regression — the queue is SERIAL (`guard activeTask == nil`,
`DownloadClient+Scheduling.swift:44`) — is the case where the blanked pages are never re-earned
by the gallery that lost them: the reconciled gallery departs part-way (pause / expiration /
failure / delete) and the ledger retires it at its honest lowered count while the floor still
holds the pre-blanking total; or a second reconciliation depresses the honest sum further. A card
whose numerator does not advance while work proceeds is the maximally stalled reading the
scheduler force-expires first, and this phase's expiration policy (D-11) turns that into a pause
of every schedulable download.

**D-G6-01, the rule this plan installs: a coordinator-made basis correction withdraws its counted
portion from the monotonic floor in the same synchronous stretch that lowers the basis — the
floor masks only movements the coordinator did not deliberately make.** The fix takes the
verifier's direction (excuse the correction from the floor rather than weakening the floor), with
two sub-decisions derived from source:

1. **The withdrawal lives inside the reconciliation itself**, after the manifest write and the
   index update, in the same non-suspending body. Whoever blanks, withdraws: the pairing is
   structural, so every route that converges on `prepareWorkingSeed` — `.repair`, the `.initial`
   reuse, the materialized repair seed — inherits it by construction, and no interleaved push can
   ever observe the lowered basis under the un-lowered floor or the reverse. The withdrawn amount
   is exactly the blanked-page count, and only when the basis was counting the record at that
   moment: the pre-blanking manifest read incomplete (its `completedPageCount` below `pageCount`,
   the same count `isIncomplete` reads), or the gid was already in
   `observedIncompleteSessionGIDs`. An untrusted complete-reading record contributed zero and
   withdraws zero — that is what keeps D-G4-01's open-at-zero ceiling guarantee closed and the
   entire 15-25 arc (Tests A–F, the 0 → 5 → 6 series) byte-identical in behavior.
2. **The subtraction is unclamped, and the session-start floor seed becomes an additive merge.**
   This plan's floor-site sweep (Table 1) found a second absorber the gap record's chain implies:
   on the canonical `retryPages` route the run is scheduled before the trailing
   `ensureContinuedSession`, so a correction can land inside the client-start main-actor hop —
   where the floor was just reset to zero — and the seed at `:226` then ASSIGNS the pre-hop
   snapshot (which still counted the blanked pages) over it, re-installing the stale-high floor.
   That is WR-03's overwrite class, which 15-25 closed for the two trust collections and left on
   the scalar because the scalar then had no hop-window writer; the withdrawal gives it one. So
   the withdrawal subtracts without clamping (inside the hop the scalar runs zero-minus-corrections;
   a negative floor is inert in the push's `max()`), and the seed becomes
   `max(snapshot value + lastPushedCompletedPageCount, 0)` — the pre-hop snapshot minus any
   hop-window corrections, clamped so a correction the snapshot never counted only under-seeds,
   which is the safe direction (a floor seeded low re-latches at the very next push; a floor
   seeded high is the defect).

Rejected shapes, so the executor does not re-derive them: weakening or deleting the floor loses
the residual defence against genuine disk regressions the doc names and lets an ordinary flush
race repaint backwards; a per-gallery floor map re-derives what `retiredSessionPages` plus the
live sum already provide, for the same observable behavior; a clamped withdrawal plus a separate
hop-window accumulator is two variables carrying one fact.

Three open review warnings sit on or beside the edited sites and are dispositioned in scope
(mandated by the gap contract): **WR-01** is FIXED (the authority's queue-scoped read unions the
running gallery — its exclusion distorts the same numerator this plan repairs, and the verifier

private, so the silent-variant revert that leaves today's suite green no longer compiles);

the `storage.validate` consequence and both consumers). The review's three info findings
(IN-01..IN-03) remain out of scope: none touches SC1–SC4, and none sits on an edited line.

Purpose: after this plan, no deliberate correction the coordinator makes can freeze the card —
the pushed numerator dips once, honestly, by exactly the corrected amount, and then every page of
real work anywhere in the queue advances it again; and the two adjacent holes in the same
