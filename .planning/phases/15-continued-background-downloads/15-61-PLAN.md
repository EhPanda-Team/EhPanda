<objective>
Close CR-02 by binding session observation credit to the queue-intent generation that produced it.

The queue-wide session remains one session per D-06, but its evidence is not gallery-lifetime evidence. A fresh retry, selected-page retry, resume, update, or enqueue generation must invalidate the predecessor's incomplete observation without erasing the queue-wide retirement ledger or the successor run's own `RunProgressBasis`. This preserves D-07's queue-mobilizing paths and D-10's current-session page accounting.

Purpose: prevent stale progress from being credited to a successor redo during the same continued session.
Output: a generation-scoped observation map and a production-shaped two-gallery regression.
</objective>
