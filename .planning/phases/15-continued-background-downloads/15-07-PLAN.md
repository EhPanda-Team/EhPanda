<assumption_delta_decision>
**Primary noun:** the continued-processing session, as the app's sole background-execution
mechanism.

**Decision: promote.** The ROADMAP's open scope question — replace or run alongside — was
answered by discuss-phase as full replacement (D-01/D-02). The generalized identity becomes
primary; the two older specific mechanisms are deleted, not demoted-and-retained.

**Accepted consequence:** no background execution at all when a session is not granted;
downloads suspend with the process and resume on next foreground, with no user-visible error.

**Invariant:** Task 1 of this plan encodes the generalized intent as a permanent automated
check, so the singular assumption cannot be silently reintroduced.
</assumption_delta_decision>
