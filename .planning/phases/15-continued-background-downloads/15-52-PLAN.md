<objective>
Close G-15-32 (warning). Confirmed verbatim in source by the round-16 verification. Recorded not for
its severity but because it is this phase's own recorded generator wearing a different face: a
double that is atomic where the seam suspends certifies reentrancy races as impossible.

**The rule, and where it is written.** `BackgroundProcessingClientSpy`'s header states the contract
every double at this seam must honour (`DownloadFeatureTestSupportTypes.swift:82-86`): the live
value is main-actor-confined, so every endpoint hops off the calling actor, and every one of its
three closures therefore yields at least once before it records — verified in source at `:287`
(`start`), `:329` (`updateProgress`) and `:358` (`finish`). The header states the cost of breaking
it in its own words: a double that is atomic where the seam suspends certifies reentrancy races as
impossible, which is how a drain suite can be green against a tail that interleaves in production.

**The break.** `BackgroundProcessingClient.unavailable`
(`DownloadContinuedSessionExpirationTests.swift:403-417`) has no suspension anywhere: `start` builds
and finishes the stream synchronously, and `updateProgress` and `finish` are empty closures. It is
one file over from the header that forbids exactly this, and nothing polices the difference — the
rule is written in one file and honoured only there.

**Why this family is the one least able to afford it.** Per
`DownloadContinuedSessionRunProofTests.swift:26-29` the `.unavailable` outcome is "the ordinary
outcome rather than an exotic one" — three of the four arms that yield the event fire inside the
store's own `start`. And the paths every case running this double drives exist SPECIFICALLY to
survive the client start's main-actor hop: `ensureContinuedSession`'s ownership re-check
(`+ContinuedSession.swift:268`), its additive floor seed (`:283`) and its merged trust seed
(`:313`), each carrying a doc that names the hop as the window it defends. With the hop removed
those three lines are executed in a world where the window they exist for cannot open, so their
coverage is nominal rather than real.

**Why a census rather than a third correct double.** The gap's own note on why the tests missed it
is that nothing polices double timing: the rule is written in one file's header and honoured only
there, and a census over the suspension points at this seam would have caught it. This round's
predecessor closed a claim that had been wrong for six consecutive rounds by making a build own it
rather than correcting it a third time; the same instrument applies here, and the same discipline
applies to it — a guard that has only ever been observed green has never been shown to reach
anything.

Purpose: the `.unavailable` family's coverage becomes real rather than nominal, and the timing rule
at this seam is enforced by a build instead of by a comment in a neighbouring file (SC3's refusal
half, SC4's seam).
Output: a suspending `.unavailable` double carrying its own reason, a two-part census owning the
rule across the target with each part separately falsified, a classified population, a per-case
re-read of what the added window changes, and a green full run.
</objective>
