<objective>
Close G-15-8 (blocker; violates SC1 — a download started in the foreground continues to
completion after backgrounding). `moveDownload` (`+Folders.swift`) inserts its gid into the
scheduling block set with a function-scoped `defer`, then suspends at `fetchDownload` and twice
at `reloadDownloadRecord`, and every one of its six exits (invalid-name aside, which precedes
the insert: not-found, busy, same-destination success, destination-exists, move-failure catch,
success) returns WITHOUT calling `scheduleNextIfNeeded()`. Its family siblings converge
explicitly under the "ACTIVE-OWNERSHIP CONVERGENCE" comment (`deleteFolder`, `delete`);
`moveDownload` is the one member the phase's five convergence rounds never swept. Two
consequences, both re-derived in the gap: while the block is held, the one authority
(`schedulableDownloads()`) cannot see the gid, so if the moved gallery is the only schedulable
work and a completion-path convergence lands in the window, `reconcileContinuedSession` reads no
pending work, pushes a terminal pair, and ends the session over work that is merely hidden; and
because the success path never converges, the moved gallery stays queued and idle until some
unrelated mutation schedules — with no fallback tier (D-03) and no session restart without a
fresh qualifying tap.

The fix, per the gap's mandated shape:

1. **The invariant, stated once and swept everywhere:** no exit may leave a gid blocked or the
   queue unconverged. `moveDownload` releases and converges (`notifyObservers()` then
   `scheduleNextIfNeeded()`) on every exit, exactly as `delete` and `deleteFolder` do. The whole
   insert set — `delete` (`+PublicAPI.swift`), `deleteFolder` and `moveDownload`
   (`+Folders.swift`), `commitPause` (`+Scheduling.swift`) — is swept against the invariant with
   a per-site disposition (commitPause's convergence is owned by its callers on every path; the
   sweep proves it rather than assumes it).
2. **WR-03, bundled by mandate.** The set is released by `defer` at four sites, every one of
   which suspends while holding it on a reentrant actor, so two overlapping same-gid operations
   release each other's blocks when the first finishes. The storage becomes a reference count —
   `schedulingBlockedGalleryCounts: [String: Int]` with `blockScheduling(gid:)` /
   `releaseScheduling(gid:)` helpers and `isSchedulableDownload` testing absence — removing the
   class rather than the instance. The conversion drops the `defer`s in favor of exactly one
   release per exit path (the Set tolerated double-removal; a count must not), verified per
   site.
3. **Scope honesty.** The mid-suspension teardown race (a convergence landing while the block is
   held) is a real window this fix narrows to the operation's own suspensions but does not
   eliminate; it is not deterministically stageable through production seams without adding
   production suspension hooks, and the recorded suggested_fix does not ask for them. The
   regressions pin what IS deterministic: every exit unblocks and converges, and overlapping
   blocks release independently. This scoping choice is recorded in the summary.

Purpose: a folder move can no longer strand a queued gallery or leave the queue unconverged, and
no overlapping operation can strip a block another operation still needs.
Output: the refcount with helpers, the exit sweep, the DEBUG seam reads, three regressions (two
observed failing first), and a green full run.
</objective>
