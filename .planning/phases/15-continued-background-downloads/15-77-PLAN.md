Deliver the second owner-directed UAT follow-up (15-UAT.md § Deferred Follow-Ups, test 6): eliminate the swipe-delete disappear–reappear–disappear sequence. Built directly from 15-RESEARCH.md § "Deferred Follow-Up Research: Swipe-Action Deletion Choreography (2026-08-10)" and its Spike Definition: standard-components-first, cheapest-first, spike-to-keep, device-UAT-gated (the Phase 3 precedent). This plan implements **Candidate 0 only** — drop `role: .destructive` from the swipe delete button (DownloadsView.swift:137-141), `.tint(.red)`, and animate the confirmed removal — then gates on an owner device evaluation. The research establishes (docs-index sweep, VERIFIED) that the full three-part hold-open choreography is IMPOSSIBLE with native `.swipeActions` through the iOS 27 beta, so Candidate 0 is deliberately the platform-conventional approximation of the UAT ask, and the checkpoint's kill criterion is exactly that gap.

Root cause (research, HIGH confidence): the first vanish and the reappearance are the `.destructive` role's optimistic row-removal animation firing before any data mutation (the reducer only sets `state.alert`; the data source is untouched); the second vanish is the genuine async observe-stream removal applied without animation. Dropping the role removes beats 1-2; animating the snapshot application choreographs beat 3.

On owner satisfaction: UAT-FU-2 closes with the decision recorded. On kill: the SUMMARY records the kill verdict and routes **Candidate 1** (the in-house custom swipe container, with the research's nine gate criteria and three kill criteria carried verbatim below for the record) to a follow-up planning round — this plan must NOT begin building it.

Purpose: close the deferral with the cheapest candidate or a recorded, decidable kill.
Output: the two-line product change plus one animation, a region-scoped source regression, and an owner checkpoint carrying the research's gate criteria verbatim.
</objective>
