<objective>
Delete the execution-assertion client and the old drain loop from `DownloadClient`, leaving
the queue with no background-execution mechanism at all.

Purpose: D-02 removes the grace-window assertion outright, and the drain loop existed only to
pump the scheduler inside a relaunched background process that no longer happens. Both are
pure deletions, and doing them before the rebuild keeps every intermediate commit green and
keeps the new session code from being written around dead scaffolding. The schedulable-work
predicate survives, decoupled from the mechanism it used to drive.

Output: `DownloadClient` with no assertion state, no assertion client, no drain loop, no
façade endpoints for either, and a relocated blocking-coordinator fixture the session tests
will reuse.

**Scope note — 15 files, deliberately not split.** This plan is above the usual ten-file
guideline. It is not split because every entry is one half of a single deletion: removing a
stored dependency, its initializer parameter, its live argument, its two call sites and its
test double are not independently buildable steps. Splitting them would leave at least one
intermediate commit with a red build, which is exactly what the deletion-before-rebuild
sequencing exists to avoid. The file count is high; the conceptual surface is one deletion.
</objective>
