## Decisions this plan makes (owner-approved design extension, 2026-08-09)

**D-SSOT-09 — scope of "displayed completeness quantity".** The families cover RECORD-completeness
displays: the badge progress (completedPageCount/pageCount), the inspector's page states and
counts, and any further consumer the enumeration in Task 1 finds. The continued-processing card's
X/Y series is explicitly OUT of scope: its numerator is a run-owned MEASURED quantity
(RunProgressBasis, the round-18 redesign) and its denominator a session-scoped ledger — those are
progress-of-this-run, not completeness-of-this-record, and unifying them into the manifest basis
would reintroduce the inference the redesign deleted. The suite's header records this boundary so
a future reader does not "fix" the distinction.

**No-dead-end, defined.** A state is non-terminal incomplete when its persisted record reads
incomplete (manifest.isIncomplete) or an operation-level validationErrors entry is present, and it
is not already moving (.active/.queued are in motion; .completed/.updateAvailable with a clean
record are terminal for this property). For every such state the suite must find at least one
forward affordance that SUCCEEDS when actually driven: `togglePause` resuming an `.inactive`
record, `retryPages` over `retryablePageIndices` for the operation-level-error shape, or
`validateImageData` followed by one of the former on the state it produces. Predicates alone are
not enough — G-15-5's dead end had predicates that all individually looked reasonable; the drive
must reach `.queued` (or a successful validate that unlocks a drive that does).
