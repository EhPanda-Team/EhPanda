<objective>
Close G-15-16 (warning; introduced in part by 15-31, whose G-15-8 convergence sweep counted two
exits that cannot happen). Verified in source: `writeInitialPauseRecord` and
`writeSettledPauseRecord` are declared async throws, but their bodies call only non-throwing
members (`clearDownloadSessionState`, the queue-store remove, the background-task-store
removeAll, `notifyObservers`), and nothing else in `commitPause`'s do block throws either — so
both catch arms are unreachable dead code while a comment asserts they are that path's single
release. Neither helper reads its `download: DownloadedGallery` parameter, and the settled
helper re-runs the initial helper's three mutations verbatim with nothing explaining what the
awaited cancelled task can have undone. Compounding it (IN-05 of the round-11 review):
`releaseScheduling` documents an unmatched release as a contract violation and then tolerates it
with a device-only log line — with two counted release sites unreachable, a future imbalance
would never surface in the suite that guards G-15-8.

The fix, per the gap's decided remedies:

1. **Let the compiler own the unreachability.** Drop `throws` and the unused parameter from both
   helpers; the do block then has no throwing member, so the dead catch arms and the do wrapper
   must go — and any future throwing addition to the pause path becomes a compile-forced,
   deliberate catch decision instead of dead scaffolding absorbing it silently. The wrong
   comment dies with the arms.
2. **Justify or delete the duplicate mutations** by tracing what the awaited cancelled task's
   teardown actually writes.
3. **Make the imbalance observable to tests**: `reportIssue` alongside the existing log,
   production behavior unchanged, pinned by a canary.

Purpose: the pause path's exit inventory is real, and the convergence contract it protects is
assertable.
Output: cleaned helpers and commitPause, the traced settled-record decision, the observable
release imbalance with its canary, and a green full run.
</objective>
