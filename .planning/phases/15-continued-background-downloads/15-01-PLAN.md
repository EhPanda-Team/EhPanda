<objective>
Retire the discretionary background-task tier from the app shell and repoint the
`Info.plist` permitted-identifier surface at continued-processing tasks.

Purpose: D-01 removes the discretionary path entirely. Nothing may register or schedule a
scheduler task at launch or on scene-phase change any more, and the plist must permit the
per-session, bundle-prefixed identifiers the rebuilt client will mint. This plan also
resolves RESEARCH.md Assumption A2 (does `$(PRODUCT_BUNDLE_IDENTIFIER)` expand inside a
nested array string?) with a `plutil -p` assertion on the built app, so no later plan
inherits it as an open risk.

Output: An `Info.plist` permitting only `$(PRODUCT_BUNDLE_IDENTIFIER).continued.*`, an
`AppFeature` free of task-scheduler wiring, and a package graph where `appFeature` no longer
depends on the client module.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Primary noun:** the continued-processing session, as the app's sole background-execution
mechanism.

**Decision: promote.** The ROADMAP's "Open for discuss-phase" line asked whether the
continued-processing task *replaces* the discretionary path or runs *alongside* it as the
user-initiated tier. Discuss-phase answered it: full replacement (D-01/D-02). The generalized
identity — "one queue-wide continued-processing session" — becomes primary and the two older
specific mechanisms are **deleted**, not demoted-and-retained.

**Accepted consequence:** when the system refuses, queues indefinitely, or expires the
session, there is no background execution at all — downloads suspend with the process and
resume on next foreground, with no user-visible error (SC3 as amended).

**Invariant:** plan 15-07 adds a permanent source-tree invariant check so a future phase
cannot silently reintroduce the singular assumption.
</assumption_delta_decision>

`EhPanda.app/Info.plist` that the build-setting token expanded to the literal

the token does not expand, the permitted identifier would be a literal dollar-sign string,
registration would return false, and every session would report unavailable. If it does not

finding in the plan summary.
