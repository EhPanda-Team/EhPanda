completeness check ahead of the work-item short-circuit — was validated against source and

## D-G4-01 — the session-basis rule this plan installs

**A schedulable gallery's session-completed page count is its record's `completedPageCount` when
the record reads incomplete or this session has ever observed it incomplete, and zero otherwise.
The per-gallery `pageCount` denominator and the schedulable `galleryCount` are untouched.**

Formally, inside `schedulableSnapshot()`:
`(download.isIncomplete || observedIncompleteSessionGIDs.contains(download.gid)) ? download.completedPageCount : 0`
— applied to the summed numerator **and** to the `finishedPages` values the ledger observes, from
the same single index read.

Why each half exists:

- `download.isIncomplete` (raw counting): the common case. Every ordinary download, and every redo
  from the moment its own manifest writes make the record incomplete — mid-run progress is counted
  the instant it exists, with no dependence on the trust set having caught up.
- `observedIncompleteSessionGIDs` (earned trust): a gallery this session has watched doing real
  work keeps counting raw even when its record reads complete again — which is precisely the
  completion flush, where the forced push reports the full count while the gallery is still inside
  its own schedulable set. Without this half, the cadence suite's pinned `[8, 16, 20]` series would
  regress to a floored final value.
- The zero branch: a complete-reading record the session has never seen incomplete. Its finished
  pages predate the session — they are the redo's *target*, not its progress. This is the gap case
  (queued `.update`/`.redownload`), and equally the repair-on-a-complete-record route, the
  active fetch window before a redo's first manifest write, and the `enqueue` route that reuses a
  complete manifest without ever touching `queuedModes`.

**The same trust set gates the retirement's record read.** D-G2-01's record-authoritative departure
rule exists so a gallery completing between two pushes retires its full count. For a redo item that
never ran, the record is authoritative about the *manifest*, not about this session's work: a
cancelled never-started update would retire N pages the session never downloaded, inflating both
sides. So `reconcileRetiredSessionPages` retires a departed gallery from its record only when the
gallery is in the trust set; otherwise it retires the last observation — zero by construction. This
is not a departure-reason branch: the formula still has no reason parameter and treats completion,
pause, delete, cancel and expiration identically; it keys only on what the session observed while
the gallery was present.

**Accepted residual, documented at the site:** a never-trusted redo that starts and finishes
entirely between two observations retires at its observed basis of zero — the module's existing
unobserved-work convention ("a gallery that both joins and fully departs between two pushes enters
neither the live sum nor the ledger"), reached from one observation further out. The safe direction
is deliberate: under-retiring keeps the fraction at or below truth; over-retiring is the defect.
