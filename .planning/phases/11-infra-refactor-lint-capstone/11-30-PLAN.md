## Diagnosis status

G-11-7 is **already diagnosed at high confidence** (`11-UAT.md` §Gaps, and
`.planning/debug/g-11-7-fetch-more-broken.md`). Do **not** re-open the investigation, and do not
spend context re-deriving the root cause. The following are established facts, not hypotheses:

- `FrontpageReducer` and its six siblings are **correct and cleared**. `fetchMoreGalleries` still
  guards on `hasNextPage()` + `footerLoadingState != .loading` and sets `.loading` synchronously
  before returning its effect, so a duplicate send is already a no-op. `insertGalleries`,
  `PageNumber.hasNextPage()`, `Gallery.id` as a stable cursor and the `lastID` plumbing are all
  cleared. **Change none of them.**
- Because the reducer already owns re-entrancy, the view needs **no view-local latch**. That is
  exactly what made the deleted per-row trigger correct without one. Adding a latch here would
  recreate the failure mode this plan is removing.
- `Setting.listDisplayMode` defaulting to `.detail` is why the blast radius is every list. It is
  not a defect. **Do not change the default.**
