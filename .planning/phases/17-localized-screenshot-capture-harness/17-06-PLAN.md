## Owner decisions: Live Text records and download page files (2026-10-09)

Before any change, the plan's feasibility check found two gaps. The selection names Live Text page
4 of the flow gallery but no text regions for it, and the fixture holds only the detail page and
cover of each download gallery, so no download folder could hold actual page files. The owner
decided:

1. **Live Text comes from a recording saved in the fixture.** The capture never runs Vision. An
   extra acquisition step records the page: the app opens the saved page in the reader on the
   iPhone visual simulator and runs Live Text through its own recognizer, request settings and
   grouping. The owner chose this over capture-time recognition and over a Mac-side Vision tool,
   because coordinates read from the running app are the ones the app produces. The recording
   leaves the app through the existing diagnostics channel (the `capture_diagnostics` value, a
   UI test attachment, the exported xcresult), not through logs, whose messages can be truncated
   and whose numbers would have to be parsed from text. The record keeps every group and every
   line with its text, four normalized corners and IDs, plus the gallery, the page, the page
   image's SHA-256, the recognition languages and the recording simulator's model and runtime. A
   new fixture version holds it. The `reading-live-text` capture checks the record's identity and
   bounds, puts its groups into `LiveTextHandler` for that page, keeps Vision off, and lets the
   real `LiveTextView` draw them. Every recognized line is drawn.
2. **Acquisition saves the download galleries' page files.** `fixtures.py` derives each download
   entry's pages: every page of a complete entry and the first `completedPages` pages of any
   other. A new acquisition saves their images, and the capture seeds capture-owned download
   folders with those files and their SHA-256 hashes.
3. **Files outside the plan's list.** The owner allowed changes beyond `files_modified`:
   `CaptureDiagnostics.swift` (which reads every reader as `reading` until Live Text is
   readable), the fixture tooling and its tests (`fixtures.py`, `acquire-fixtures.py`,
   `test_fixtures.py`), the app's fixture manifest decoding, and the recording step.
