## Owner approval: owned-row verification and full-content settle (2026-10-05)

The owner explicitly approved the three-point remedy for the tracer-matrix-05 failure
("Approve all three") and declined a runner quiet period ("No wait"). This amends
the same-rect approval above; every other rule in it stays in force.

Measured basis. Standalone probes on a temporary iPhone Air simulator (iOS 27.0,
landscape 912 x 420 pt at 3x, AX2, light) read MiscGridItem replicas at the failing
geometry through the production read path. The evidence is copied byte-identically
to the durable, untracked `.capture/research/glass-reread/` (RESULTS.md, logs and
probe sources). On a quiet simulator, settled same-rect rereads are byte-identical
(300+ reads per rect; 18 rect variants x 40 interleaved reads; read order never
matters). Glass that first renders after window growth or appearance changes once
within about 0.1 to 0.45 s (up to 67 px, maximum delta 2). Under system load (after
boot; the tracer recreates it per coordinate through content_size changes and fresh
test hosts), glass cut by a read's bottom edge flips one rim pixel by one level,
sporadically, for seconds. Every flip lay within 9 pt of the cut (4.7 to 5.7 pt
observed), including the native rect's cut rows. No load flip was seen for whole
glass, top cuts or deep-cut rows. The tracer-matrix-05 pixel matches the load flip;
the missing full-content settle is a second exposure. These are measured
observations; the render-server mechanism is not established.

Rules:

1. Each read's two passes compare SHA-256 over the rows it contributes to the
   composite, in canonical 8-bit sRGB RGBA. Read 0 is the native rect and owns all
   its rows; the final read owns only rows inside the content. Context and overflow
   rows exist to keep read edges away from composed output and are not gated.
   Whole-read digests are still recorded for both passes as evidence only. An
   owned-row mismatch throws readChanged and saves both whole reads.
2. After layout convergence and before the verified passes, the grown window settles
   under the native viewport rule: repeat single passes of owned-row digests until
   consecutive passes are identical over a run spanning at least 500 ms, at most 40
   passes, else the dedicated `fullContentRenderDidNotSettle` error. Every pass is
   recorded in `full-content-settle.json`. The native viewport and full-content
   settles share one implementation of this rule.
3. No quiet period is added after content_size changes or launches. Read 0's rows,
   including those at the native viewport edge, stay fully gated; a flip there fails
   the capture loudly with evidence.

Native gate and runner evidence validation check the new ledger fields: owned rows
must match each read's geometry, read 0 must own every row with equal owned and
whole-read digests, owned digests must match across passes, whole-read match flags
must agree with their digests, and the settle ledger must record an accepted run.
Tests cover context/overflow changes that pass, owned-row and read-0 edge changes
that fail, single-pass digests, and the settle rule's reset, acceptance and bound
with an injected clock. The RenderServerCapture citation of wiped /tmp evidence is
replaced by a citation of these planning records.
