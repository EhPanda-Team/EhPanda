<assumption_delta_decision>
The orchestrator's assumption-delta scan fired two signals: `pluralization` on "every color-coded state *also* carries a shape, glyph, or text" (ROADMAP criterion 11) and `chosen` on "four error-level SwiftLint *custom* rules" (criterion 6). Evaluated honestly: the first is a prose cue ("also" adds a second carrier to an existing state, not a second identity), and the second is SwiftLint vocabulary (`custom_rules` is the config key), not a constant becoming a parameter.

- **Noun now primary:** none changes. The one genuine identity question in this phase is D-26: the category badge *text* colour stops being a constant (`.white`) and becomes a value **derived** from the resolved background luminance. The background remains the single primary identity of a category colour (frozen byte-identical, 84 variants); the foreground is a pure function of it and is never stored, chosen, or persisted anywhere.
- **Decision:** `no-change` — the derived foreground is promoted nowhere and demoted nothing; it is computed at render time from the existing primary. No add-alongside debt is created: there is no second "text colour" source of truth to drift from the background.
- **Companion invariant (accepted, planned):** `CategoryColorsetInvariantTests` (plan 16-14) pins the 44 standard background variants byte-for-byte and asserts best-of-black/white contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for all 84 — the test that encodes "background is the identity, foreground is derived".
</assumption_delta_decision>
