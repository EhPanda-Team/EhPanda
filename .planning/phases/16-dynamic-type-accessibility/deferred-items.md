## Found during 16-21

- `AppPackage/Sources/DownloadsFeature/DownloadsView+Subviews.swift` `DownloadInspectorValidationActionLabel`: the spinner's `.transition(…)` (now `reduceMotion ? .opacity : .opacity.combined(with: .scale(scale: 0.85))`) sits on a view whose visibility is `visible(isValidating)`, i.e. an `opacity` toggle — the `ProgressView` is never inserted or removed, so no transition (scale or opacity) ever plays. A downloads owner deciding the fate of `progressAnimation` above should decide this with it (an `if isValidating { ProgressView() }` would make the transition real; or drop the transition).
