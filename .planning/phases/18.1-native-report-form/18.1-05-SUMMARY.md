   - **Run 1.** Proxyman's Block List, plus Map Local for the saved pages. STOP at step 5: the block wins at the
     CONNECT tunnel before Map Local can answer.

     Proxyman opened short TCP connections to real site addresses.

     re-read.

     again in run 2 is unknown, because the session was cleared first.

   Homebrew on the Mac, on the lead's decision. Nothing was added to the repository.

   - While each run's window was open (about 70 s, three times), other Mac processes also reached mitmdump. Apple

     not been recorded beforehand, so restoration is checked by its rows.
9. **"A gallery and its comments" (owner msg 1997).** A gallery's comments come from its own page. For the unsaved
   gallery that page was answered 403, so the comments list had nothing to open. The request that would have loaded
