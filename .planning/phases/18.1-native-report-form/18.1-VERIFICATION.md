**Failures found, none in 18.1 code:**

- **GalleryCard (fixed, `7b81e242a`).** The test tapped the Settings tab by its glyph's identifier,
  `gearshape.circle`, which the tab bar no longer listed on the phone (`exists: false`; the tab itself was on screen).
  It failed alone too. It now taps the tab by its label, as `DuoToolbarUITests` does. Alone afterwards: 3/3.
- **DeepLinkScheme page links on the pad (gap, not fixed).** Both failed all three attempts on clone 4, after
  `GalleryCardUITests` on the same clone. The recording shows the reader in **landscape** with a two-page spread
  (pages 2 | 1) and "1 / 156", the known iPad spread limit (Phase 18 DD-03). Alone (the class, portrait) they pass
  8/8, and `testGridAligns…` followed by the page link in separate runs also passed. The likely cause is a leaked
  orientation: `GalleryCardUITests.testGridAligns…` turns the device to landscape and restores the orientation it
  read at the start, which on a freshly booted clone may not be portrait. Not reproduced, so not fixed.
- **Live connections from FeatureTests (gap).** A socket sampler on `xctest` saw TCP 443 connections to
  `172.66.132.196` / `2606:4700:3035::ac43:bbdb` (e-hentai.org's Cloudflare addresses) from four test processes during
  the **pad** FeatureTests run, 01:08:14Z to 01:09:58Z: the AppFeature, DownloadsFeature, HomeFeature and
  SettingFeature test bundles (mapped by process ID to their suites). None was seen during the phone run. The
  tests passed, so a test leans on a live dependency somewhere. Not in 18.1 code; not diagnosed.
- **UI tests' app sockets:** 0 non-loopback sockets from the app in the pad run; the phone run showed only the UI
