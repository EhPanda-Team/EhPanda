# Phase 18.1 Plan 03: Report form reducer and routing Summary

**Report on a comment now hands the screen a ready-to-present Report form instead of an alert, and the form's whole
C-13 behaviour is proven without a view. The form loads the site's form, offers two reasons, holds the email only in
memory, posts once, and on failure keeps everything and offers Retry or the site's page. Nothing is visible yet:
18.1-04 draws the sheet.**

## Accomplishments

- **Reducer, client and prefill (Task 1, `263609119`):**
  - `ReportFormFeature` carries the comment (author, date, text), the site, the gallery URL and its report URLs.
    Phases: loading → ready / unavailable, ready → sending → sent, or back to ready with the failed alert. Reasons
    are exactly [child, illegal] → `report_type` child / other. Nothing is picked on open.
  - It loads `?report=other` and sends to `?report=<type>`, both rebuilt from the gallery URL. It offers the site
    page `?report=select` from the unavailable state and from the alert.
  - `primaryTapped` is one action for the pinned button and ⌘↩: send, nothing while sending, dismiss when sent, or
    open the site page when unavailable.
  - The failed alert is `report.failed.title` / `report.failed.body({site})` with Retry, Open Report Page
    (`comment.report.open`) and Cancel (`cancel`, role cancel), in that order.
  - `ReportFormClient.liveValue` calls `ReportFormRequest` and `SendReportRequest` on the shared session. Its
    `testValue` is unimplemented.
  - `ReportPrefill` gives `Reporting a comment by {name} ({date}):\n"{text}"\n\n` with `yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm 'UTC'`.
  - Threats: the email is only in the state. Nothing reads `UserDefaults`, `@Shared`, the keychain or the analytics
    client (grep 0), and a full flow under the unimplemented analytics client passes (T-18.1-05, D-11). The
    prefill is plain text in `r_details`, which the user sees and can edit (T-18.1-06, accepted). No package was
    installed (T-18.1-SC).
- **Routing (Task 2, `f7a389135`):**
  - `reportTapped` on a foreign comment sends `delegate(.openReportForm(state))`. Own comments and galleries with
    no report target send nothing.
  - `Alert.confirmReport`, `Delegate.openReportPage` and the alert's use of `commentReportConfirmTitle` / `Body`
    are gone (grep 0).
  - Comments and Detail set `destination = .reportForm(state)`. Their existing `ifLet` runs the form, and the form's
    `dismiss` clears the destination.
  - `reportPage(URL)` stays in both destinations, unused, so this plan changes no view. 18.1-04 removes it.
