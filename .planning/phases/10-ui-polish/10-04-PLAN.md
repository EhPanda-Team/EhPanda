<objective>
Remove the custom `\.inSheet` environment key (ROADMAP criterion 6; 13 refs: 1 definition, 5 setters, 3 consumer sites in 2 files) and reimplement its presentation-context logic — all three consumers only pick gray tones — via the native trait mechanism: a `UIColor` dynamic provider keyed on `traitCollection.userInterfaceLevel` (UIKit passes `.elevated` to modally presented content, light and dark alike). Mechanism per RESEARCH recommendation; owner-gated at a D-11 checkpoint because the trait approach carries one known behavioral delta.

**The delta, stated explicitly (Pitfall 4):** the old key was only set on 5 specific sheets. Other sheets that render these surfaces (QuickSearch, Filters, Favorites' modal detail, deep-link modal detail) currently resolve the NON-sheet gray and will resolve the ELEVATED gray under the trait approach. That is arguably bug-fixed behavior (missed setters), but it is a visible change from today. The exact-parity fallback — threading an explicit `Bool` init parameter from the 5 former setter sites — reintroduces the parameter drilling Phase 7 removed; it stays theoretical unless the owner rejects the delta at the checkpoint.

Purpose: criterion 6; deletes a hand-rolled mechanism the trait system models natively.
Output: environment key deleted, trait-based grays at 3 consumer sites, privacyMask coverage intact, owner sign-off on the mechanism.
</objective>
