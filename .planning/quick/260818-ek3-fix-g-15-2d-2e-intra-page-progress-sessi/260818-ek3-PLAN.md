## Planner design decisions (Claude's Discretion — justified, do not re-litigate)

**PD-1 Sub-page fold lives in the client seam, not in the coordinator's pair (the "equivalent design").** `BackgroundProcessingClient.updateProgress` gains a fifth argument `inFlightSubunitCount: Int64` (per-mille of a page, summed over in-flight pages), and `ContinuedProcessingSession` — the owner of the system `Progress` — scales: `totalUnitCount = total * subunitsPerUnit`, `completedUnitCount = min(completed * subunitsPerUnit + inFlight, total * subunitsPerUnit)`, `subunitsPerUnit = 1000`. Why not scale inside the coordinator: 83 assertion sites across 8 suites pin the pushed PAGE series (`completedUnitCount == 5`, `[0, 10, 16, 20]`, `expectTheCompletedSeriesNeverLosesGround`), and every one of them is a hard-won G-15-6/7/34 pin; scaling the coordinator's pair would re-baseline all of them and fold the sub-page term INTO the monotonic floor whose writer census (`expectedFloorWriters` = 4+1) and doc block forbid an incidental fold. Keeping the coordinator's pair in pages means: no new writer of `lastPushedCompletedPageCount`, D-G2-01/D-G7-01/D-G2C-01 untouched, and the client stays domain-agnostic ("already-clamped counts" plus one more count). Blast radius: the client (2 files), its own suite (~15 scaled assertions), the spy record (+1 field), two hand-built doubles' closure arity, and one `ProgressUpdate(` init in tests.

**PD-2 Sub-page credit is a run fact keyed (gid → page), lifetime = first byte → the flush that records the page.** An in-flight entry is created at transfer start (`rawPageDownloadResponse`), grows by `max` (a retry restarts bytes at 0 but the page's credit never rewinds while in flight), is REMOVED inside `flushManifestPageProgress` in the same synchronous stretch as `outstandingPages.subtract` (so whole-page credit +1000 and sub-page credit −≤1000 trade places atomically — no dip between a page landing and its flush), is withdrawn by name at the page's `.failure` outcome (the deliberate mover the CONTEXT allows; a sub-page drop of < 1 page, documented, outside the whole-page floor), and the gallery's map is dropped at `retireRunProgressBasis` under its existing supersession gate. It is NOT session-scoped (same reasoning as `runProgressBases`, G-15-26). The pushed sum covers only gids present in the same snapshot the pair was computed from (`snapshot.finishedPages.keys`) so it cannot credit a gallery whose pages left the denominator.

**PD-3 Liveness reaches the pump through the download client, consumed by AppReducer, not SettingFeature.** New endpoint `DownloadClient.observeContinuedSessionLiveness: @Sendable () -> AsyncStream<Bool>` (default finish-immediately like `observeDownloads`), published `true` when `continuedClientSessionID` lands and `false` in `markContinuedSessionEnded`. AppReducer already imports DownloadClient and owns the pump; it keeps `isContinuedSessionLive` in state and decides. A `@Shared` flag was rejected: `Reducer.onChange` cannot see a change made outside a reduce, so an effect would be needed anyway, and a stream endpoint is testable through the client double.

**PD-4 "created foreground|background" comes from a new `DownloadClient.setIsInBackground: @Sendable (Bool) async -> Void`** driven from AppReducer's `.active`/`.background` arms (unconditionally, before the settings-loaded guard). DownloadClient stays UIKit-free; the value is a Bool the coordinator stamps onto each transfer at start.

**PD-5 Stall (≥10 s zero bytes) is detected at two points, ONE log helper:** the heartbeat tick sweeps in-flight transfers, and `endPageTransfer` (the `defer` around the downloader call) checks on exit — so a transfer cancelled by the expiry pause sweep after starving logs how long it starved. Both call the same `logStarvedPageTransfer(...)` so the privacy census counts one masked site for it.

**PD-6 Files.** Heartbeat + environment logging → new `DownloadClient+ContinuedSessionHeartbeat.swift`; in-flight credit + telemetry → new `DownloadClient+PageTransferProgress.swift`; liveness stream → new `DownloadClient+ContinuedSessionLiveness.swift`; env probe → new `DownloadEnvironmentProbe.swift`. `DownloadClient+ContinuedSession.swift` is at 970/1000 lines and `+Manager.swift` at 917/1000: only stored properties (with SHORT docs, ≤5 lines each) go into Manager, and only call-site lines (≤ 15 total) into ContinuedSession; docs go in the new files. `DownloadFeatureTestHelpers.swift` is at 992/1000: DO NOT add to it — new suites carry their own private fixture builders. Tests for the LogsClient actor live in `SettingFeatureTests` (LogsClient has no test target; its only consumer's suite is there).
