# Phase quick-261123-355 Plan 05: Row fill and switch track Summary

Rows and switches now take the system pointer highlight in their own shape: `cellRowHover` grows its view past the content, carries the hover there and shrinks back, so nothing moves at rest on the iPhone (portrait and landscape) or the iPad, and `AppToggle` applies the 61 x 28 track shape once for all 25 switches.

## Commit

- `007c4704c` `hover: add row fill and switch track` (author and committer dates `+0800`, signed, 5 files, 246 insertions). One commit since `plan_head_before`. `ReadingSettingView` numstat before the commit was `7 0`; the raw Toggle line is unchanged.

## The 4 pt shift (why the helper grows instead of clearing the insets)

The first implementation cleared the list's row insets and padded the content by the measured 20 and 15. Its first P5a compare changed four pictures: `settings-pages-reading` and `reader-settings-sheet`, en-large, iPhone landscape, light and dark (`p05/phone.failed-1`, `logs/p05-compare-phone.failed-1.log`; counts line `p05 phone en-large passed=534 recorded=0 flaky=2 changed=4 …`). In those pictures the five Picker rows' content sat 4 pt further inward on both sides (label at x=104 against 100) and the Match Screen Orientation row, which has no helper padding, did not move. The same shift was read in the iPad settings sheet in portrait (rows with the first helper start at x=163 against 159 for the row without it). The side margin in those windows is read off the pictures as 16 pt (20 - 4); what the system derives it from is not established. These facts are in the doc comment of `CellHoverFill.growth`. The probe measured the iPhone in portrait and one iPad window only, so narrow iPad windows are unmeasured.

A-05-3 replaced the technique: the row's view grows by a fixed amount, carries the hover and takes the amount back, so its layout size is the content's own in any window, and the list's insets are not touched.
