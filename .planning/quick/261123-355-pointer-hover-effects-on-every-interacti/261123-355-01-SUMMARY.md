## Environment notes (reused by plans 02 to 07)

The `.capture/261123-355/` environment (two `(261123-355)` simulators, base worktree at `base/`, runner copy, class list, candidates) is gitignored. The runner copy `run-snapshot-local.sh` differs from the tracked script in four ways:

1. `ROOT=` and `UDID=` come from `EHPANDA_ROOT` and `EHPANDA_UDID` (the tracked script would pick another task's simulator).
2. `-parallel-testing-enabled NO` on the iPad orientation-turn command.
3. `PARALLEL=(-parallel-testing-enabled NO)` for an iPad landscape capture.

Three facts about a newly created simulator, found while recording the candidate, all repaired by the orchestrator outside the plan:

- **Reduce Motion warm-up:** a fresh simulator drops Reduce Motion on its first boot (every picture errored with `configuration-reduce-motion`). Both plan simulators were booted once, had `ReduceMotionEnabled` and `ApplicationAccessibilityEnabled` written, were rebooted and read back.
- **Full Screen Apps:** a new iPad simulator starts in Windowed Apps, whose 20 pt bottom inset fails every iPad pin on `geometry`. `SBMedusaMultitaskingEnabled` was set to NO on the plan iPad.
- **No clones for the turn and the landscape capture:** parallel-testing clones always boot in portrait, so the turn rotated a throwaway clone and the landscape capture ran in portrait. The runner copy now passes `-parallel-testing-enabled NO` for both, as `Scripts/visual/run-shard.sh` does for the hosted gate.

Failed attempts are kept beside the good ones as `*.failed-1` and `p01/phone.launchfail-1`.
