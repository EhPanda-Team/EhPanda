## Pipeline per page

Runs in `LiveTextHandler` (existing `@Observable @MainActor` owner of Live Text) whenever Live Text
recognizes a page, heavy steps in `@concurrent` static functions, as recognition does today:

1. Groups: recognized (today) or, in a capture, replayed.
2. Source text per group: lines joined in Vision's order; no separator between two CJK characters
   (Han, Kana, full-width punctuation), a line-end hyphen before a lowercase letter removed, otherwise
   one space; whitespace collapsed. All-caps Latin lettering (at least 4 cased letters, at least 80%
   uppercase) is sentence-cased first ("I" kept). A group with no letters (numbers, "!!", "...") is
   skipped and left unpainted.
3. Languages: source = the gallery language when it maps to exactly one translation language;
   otherwise `NLLanguageRecognizer` over the page's text, constrained to the gallery's candidates
   when it has several (Chinese: zh-Hans or zh-Hant). Nothing identifiable: failure. Target per P-5;
   a source equivalent to the reader's first preferred language ends the pipeline (highlight look).
4. Availability: installed: translate. Supported: publish a download request (`TranslationSession.
   Configuration`) that `ReadingView`'s `.translationTask` turns into the system prompt via
   `prepareTranslation()`, at most once per pair per activation; translate after it succeeds.
   Unsupported: failure.
5. Translate: one `translations(from:)` batch per page, `clientIdentifier` = group ID. A response
   equal to its source (an untranslatable name or SFX) leaves that group unpainted.
6. Layout (off the main actor): geometry, fill and fitting below, in page pixel space.
7. Publish `translatedPages[page]`; the view switches that page to the translated look.

Cancellation follows today's `cancelRequests()`. An activation is one period of Live Text being on;
turning it off and on again re-arms failure toasts and download prompts but keeps finished pages.

Capture determinism (Q3): in Debug, a launch with a `LiveTextCapture` installed never runs steps 2 to
7 unless the capture supplies translations, which no capture does yet. The capture session adds a
translation source to `LiveTextCapture` and feeds its replay in at step 5 (handoff notes in the
SUMMARY); normalization, geometry, fill and fitting then run live on the replayed text.

## Geometry

In page pixels (aspect matters for angles): per line, angle from top-left to top-right and height
from top-left to bottom-left. Group angle = circular mean; |angle| <= 45 degrees keeps it, steeper
text (for example vertical CJK lettering) is laid out horizontally in its axis-aligned bounds. The
oriented rectangle is the projection of all corners on the group's axes. With L = median line height:
text area = rectangle + 0.15 L per side, painted plate = rectangle + 0.3 L per side, corner radius
0.35 L (at most half the shorter side).

## Fill (Q2)

Page drawn once into an sRGB RGBA8 bitmap, long side at most 1024 px. Samples every ~2 px on two
loops 0.15 L and 0.4 L outside the plate, inside the page. Uniform when at least 85% of samples lie
within 0.06 (normalized RGB distance) of the per-channel median: fill = median, text = black or white,
whichever has the higher WCAG contrast. Otherwise: white plate, shadow blur 0.4 L at 30% black,
black text. The 85% share (not a variance) keeps a balloon's outline from flipping a white balloon to
a plate. Page content does not follow dark mode, like the page image itself.

## Fitting

CoreText measures and draws, so what is measured is exactly what is drawn: system font, semibold,
with the target language set on the attributed string so CJK glyphs come from the right regional
font; centered, line-height multiple 1.0, word wrap (UAX #14 breaking for CJK). A size fits an area
when `CTFramesetterSuggestFrameSizeWithConstraints` stays within its height, the frame shows the whole
string, and (scripts with spaces) no word is wider than the area.

- Group cap c = L divided by the font's line-height ratio; page cap C = median c.
- Floor F = min(C, 2% of the page width), legible after the reader scales the page.
- Cascade at size s: fits at s; else at 0.85 s; else at 0.85 s in the area grown 1.25 times about its
  center (the plate grows with it); else truncated with an ellipsis at 0.85 s in the grown area.
- A group that does not fit at 0.85 F in its grown area is "hopeless" and does not constrain S.
- S = the largest s in [F, C] (binary search) at which every other group fits within the first
  three cascade steps. Every group then renders at S or 0.85 S (O-1).
- Translated text is page content, scaled with the page; Dynamic Type does not apply to it, as it
  does not apply to the image. Documented in code (deliberate design).

## Rendering and interaction

`LiveTextView` reads `LiveTextHandler` from the environment (optional, so previews and unrelated
hosts fall back to the highlight). With a translated page its Canvas draws, per group, under a
transform (pixels to view points, translate to center, rotate): the plate, then the text frame (y
flipped for CoreText); the focused group gets today's accent outline. Otherwise it draws today's
highlight exactly as now (moved into a private function, unchanged). The `HighlightView` tap overlay
serves both looks: a tap focuses the group and opens the system translation popover through the
existing `_translate:` call, which shows the full translation of a truncated group (O-2).

VoiceOver: the reader's pages are one accessibility element (owner, 2026-10-11) that already hides
the Live Text overlay; the translated look keeps that.
