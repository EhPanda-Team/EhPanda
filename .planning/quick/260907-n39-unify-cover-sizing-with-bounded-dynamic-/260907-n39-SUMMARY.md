# Bounded cover sizing and stable Dynamic Type layout

## Result

- Added shared compact, standard and hero cover roles: 90/120/150pt base heights,
  one headline-relative scale bounded to 1...1.25, and 8:11 containers.
- Added a shared cover renderer with stable loading geometry, neutral backing, and 5pt clipping.
  Lists, history, rankings, the frontpage cover wall and detail use fit; the home hero explicitly uses fill.
- Replaced the detailed list's unbounded cover growth and half-row width budget with the standard role.
  Preserved its narrow accessibility stacking rule and aligned horizontal rows at their top edges.
- The carousel now reserves four title lines and measures intrinsic height rather than shrinking artwork
  into a viewport cap. A single-pass Layout resolves narrow accessibility stacking and wide horizontal
  placement, with a fitting compact-rating fallback. Paging IDs, margins and gradient callbacks remain intact.
- The frontpage cover wall's outer height scales with its covers. Masonry and long-image cropping are unchanged.

## Verification

- Changed-file SwiftLint and git diff whitespace checks passed, with no rule suppression.
- Native fixture builds and the final restored EhPanda Debug simulator build succeeded.
- Final regression run succeeded: two test functions, including eight parameterized card cases,
  and all twelve Dynamic Type sizes for each of the three cover roles.
- SwiftUI hosting regressions cover every Dynamic Type size for the three cover roles and compare
  short/long title heights at standard, xxxLarge, AX1 and AX5 sizes, at phone and tablet card widths.
  Hosting-size expectations account for pixel rounding of fractional point dimensions.
- Real app screenshots on iPhone Air and iPad Pro 11 simulators (iOS 26.5): default-size home,
  AX5 home in light/dark appearance, and AX5 detailed lists. Synthetic local artwork isolates layout
  from networking. The initial iPad carousel retained a provisional tall estimate; the single-pass
  layout removed that excess space in the confirmation capture.
- Evidence is outside the repository in /tmp/ehpanda-cover-review/ (phone-ax5-final.png,
  ipad-ax5-final.png, phone-list-ax5.png, ipad-list-ax5.png, and initial default captures).
  Fixture lettering in the AX5 captures is clipped inside the generated source raster, not by fit sizing.
- A temporary test-runner screenshot experiment could not reliably host lazy views and was removed.
  Visual verification used a temporary app entry point instead; the normal entry point was restored.

## Limits and cleanup

- Simulator verification is not physical-device verification. No exhaustive landscape, multitasking,
  VoiceOver, or every-screen sweep was performed. Detail/history/ranking changes compile and share the
  verified renderer, but their full interaction flows were not individually walked in this quick task.
- Short carousel titles intentionally leave reserved space to keep paging height stable; long titles
  truncate after four preview lines and the gallery destination provides the complete title.
- The temporary app entry point and capture test are not shipped. Original simulator settings
  (large text size, light appearance) were restored. The verified normal app was reinstalled on both snapshot simulators.
- Quick ran inline and sequentially under the dispatch gate's documented fallback. No subagent
  execution or model-setting claim is made. No push or roadmap change.
